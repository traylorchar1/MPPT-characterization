Points

Read with care. The Photon parks its input where its own tracker decides (29-60 V here), and dithers about +/-1 V, so single points scatter by about 1 pt; the 0.25 A point (101%) is noise. Instrument accuracy (XPF current, load current) is about 1-2 pts at low power. The TPEE was measured with its input held (pure conversion); the Photon number includes its tracking. The TPEE drew essentially nothing from its 12 V supply; the Photon draws ~0.15 A (1.9 W).
